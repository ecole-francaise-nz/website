# 🚀 2027 Project: Extension of Our Bilingual Pathway (Years 6 to 9) 🇫🇷🇳🇿

École Française Internationale Auckland is currently exploring the possibility of opening new classes for the 2027 school year. 📚✨

In addition to the planned CM2 (Year 6) class, we are studying the opportunity to offer 6ème (Year 7), 5ème (Year 8), and 4ème (Year 9), continuing the French curriculum taught in a bilingual environment.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 We invite all interested families to complete our [Expression of Interest form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1eBZfhQUeYiR4Qy-Ck_k94bfVWf2XmfCr1ZlOfeOgTlY/viewform). Your participation will help us:

* ✅ Assess demand
* ✅ Plan the project effectively
* ✅ Keep you informed as the proposal develops

Together, we can continue to build a strong and inspiring bilingual school for our students! 🌍🎓

Thank you for your support and participation. 🙏
